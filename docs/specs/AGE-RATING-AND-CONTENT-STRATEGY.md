# Fictional content and audience

Psychosims is an interactive fictional game, not therapy or medical treatment.
Patient conditions, medication names and scenarios use the invented taxonomy.
Player copy and generated dialogue must not offer real clinical guidance.
No real health information is needed to play.

The Manipulative card is a consequential fictional gamble. Keep its language
strategic and non-degrading; show adverse outcomes rather than providing a
real-world manipulation tutorial. Psychological distress remains stylized,
without graphic self-harm or sexualized/degrading patient depiction.

The existing disclaimer/copy is owned by `app/lib/shared/l10n.dart` and checked
alongside the bundled manifest. [Content-integrity rules](../code/CONTENT_INTEGRITY.md)
and output validation apply to every accepted model reply. There is no public
user-content submission or player-message service in this release.

Psychological themes may require a mature audience. No ESRB, PEGI, IARC, Apple
or regional age rating has been obtained for this web build. Any future store
submission requires its own current questionnaire and review; obsolete native
store checklists do not establish a rating.
